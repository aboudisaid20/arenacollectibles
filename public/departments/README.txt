Department pictures for the "Three ways in" section on the home page.

Drop a file in here named after the department:

    wax.jpg       -> Sealed Boxes
    card.jpg      -> Cards
    signed.jpg    -> Signed Memorabilia
    supplies.jpg  -> Supplies

.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp and .avif all work.

Nothing else to change - the site picks the file up on the next page
refresh. Until a file exists, that department falls back to the artwork
of a representative product.

Landscape, roughly 4:3, at least 1200px wide. Images are cropped to fill
the tile, from the centre.
